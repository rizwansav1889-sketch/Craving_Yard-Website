Big flavours, bold cravings

We only cook what we'd queue for.

Craving Yard started with one simple promise — make the kind of food you genuinely crave. Blistered pizzas with bubbling cheese, smash burgers stacked tall, golden fries, saucy wings and paratha rolls bursting with flavour. Every order is made fresh, in-house.

Slow-cooked sauces, overnight dough, no shortcuts. From a quick solo bite to a full family feast, there's a craving here waiting for every appetite.
